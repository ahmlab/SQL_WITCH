# The story bible (prompt 07): drafts for the team

These five documents are **drafts for humans to rewrite**. Nothing here is wired into the game.
The themes are fixed by the plan (`docs/plan/00_MASTER_PLAN.md` §1, §5); the words are the
team's. Each document ends with **questions for the team**.

| Document | What it decides |
|---|---|
| [01_themes.md](01_themes.md) | What the game says, which mechanics say it, what the player does that proves it. |
| [02_world.md](02_world.md) | The Kingdom of Codd: every region, its problem, people, guardian, concept, palette, music. |
| [03_cast.md](03_cast.md) | The protagonist (D2/D3), three companions, the family and its letters. |
| [04_outline.md](04_outline.md) | Scene by scene: Act 1 in full, Acts 2–3 by beats. Scene ids become script nodes. |
| [05_automaton_and_null.md](05_automaton_and_null.md) | The finale: the Automaton's drift, NULL's model, the last battle, two endings. |

## What the current game already establishes (keep for continuity)

- The protagonist (default **Ada**, any name and look since prompt 06) is an analyst who dies at
  her desk at 11:58 pm while a quarterly report nobody reads runs to 99%. She thinks SQL is
  "busywork". She wakes in a glade under a sky that is a table.
- **The Grimoire** is a floating book that teases; magic is written, not waved; every spell is a
  query; the right page holds four rows (the Rule of the Page).
- **The Training Dummy** (straw, judgemental) runs the tutorial and returns as the challenge board.
- **The Asterisk Slime** (LIMIT) wobbles out of the bushes; it melts "into a puddle of rows".
- **Sir Wherewell**, Knight of the Filter, guards the **Filter Keep** with a Ward of Relevance:
  only a spell of his weakness gets through (WHERE).
- The guide already names the next guardians: the Sorting Sage, the Twin Archivists, Count
  Aggregor.
- The world reacts to any SQL (reactions, Rewind, the Codex, Tricks), and guardians plead for
  mercy when you keep hitting them after they fell: the tone is funny first.

## Review gate

The three authors sign off on each document (a line at its end: name, date, "approved" or
"rewrite"). Then the team writes the scenes as `.script` files (prompt 03's format), one region
at a time, with the builder as an editor. Prompts 08 and 09 wait for this sign-off.
